## Platform Engineer

### Position Overview

- **Location**: Austin, TX (preferred)
- **Job Type**: Full-time
- **Work Environment**: Hybrid
  Remote with regular in-person meetings in Austin,
  Two days a week in our Austin Office  

### Key Responsibilities

- Own and optimize **[GitHub Actions](GitHubActions.md) workflows** with 
  parallel execution and comprehensive testing strategies
- Maintain local development environments and ensure parity with GitHub Actions
  using scripting tools such as **Python, bash, [justfiles](justfiles.md), 
  or makefiles**
- Provide technical guidance and education to developers on platform tools and
  best practices
- Design and implement **multi-cloud infrastructure (AWS, GCP, and Azure)**
  using **Terraform** and **Kubernetes**
- Manage complex integrations with **machine learning APIs and services**
- Maintain and upgrade **secrets management, service mesh, and networking
  architecture** across cloud providers
- Deploy and configure **observability platforms** using 
  **[OpenTelemetry (OTel)](OpenTelemetry.md)**
- [Correlate](CorrelatingMetrics.md) **machine learning and LLM metrics** with 
  traditional application observability
- Implement security controls aligned with **SOC 2, ISO 27001, and GDPR**
  requirements
- Design **asset-based permissions and access control systems**
- Maintain audit trails and documentation for regulated deployments

### Basic Qualifications

- 5+ years of experience in platform engineering, full-stack development,
  DevOps, or SRE roles
- Strong experience with cloud infrastructure (**AWS required**, GCP/Azure a
  plus)
- Proficiency with **Infrastructure as Code (Terraform strongly preferred)**
- **Python with type hints** for tooling and automation
- Experience with **CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions preferred)**
- Solid understanding of **containerization (Docker, Kubernetes)**
- Strong grasp of **networking, security, and secrets management**

### Useful Experience

- Experience with **regulated industries** (pharmaceutical, medical device,
  finance)
- Background with **compliance frameworks** (SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR)
- **Observability platform experience** using OTel (Prometheus, Grafana, or
  modern alternatives)
- Interest in modern development tools 
  - [uv](uv.md)  
  - [mise](mise.md)  
  - [devpod](devpod.md)  
  - [justfiles](justfiles.md)  
- Experience with **LLM, vision, and other machine learning frameworks**
- Knowledge of **pharmaceutical manufacturing or quality systems**
- Exposure to **[Rust](Rust.md), uv, justfile**, or other cutting-edge platform 
  tools  
  
### What Success Looks Like  
  
- Own development environments that ensure every developer is highly productive
- Maintain high uptime of CI/CD pipelines, ensuring code can always be delivered
  to test environments
- Ensure customer environments and internal environments are closely aligned to
  prevent deployment failures
- Communicate actively about tasks, dependencies, and required work from other
  developers
